WHY I WANTED TO ANIMATE

I wanted to make actually appropriate animations

Minecraft was the starting point. Animation became the way to turn ideas into something I could actually see.

THE START

Watching wasn't enough.

I got interested in animation when I was younger. I wanted to make my own animations, but when I first tried Blender, it felt way too complex for me. There were so many tools and things to learn that I didn't really know where to start.

So I decided to try something easier: Minecraft animation.

I already knew Minecraft, and it gave me a much simpler way to start creating. Instead of having to learn an entire 3D program from scratch, I could focus more on the actual animation and the ideas I wanted to make.

Watching other people make Minecraft animations was cool, but eventually watching wasn't enough. I wanted to make my own. I wanted to create animations that were actually appropriate for what I wanted to make, with my own ideas, characters, and stories.

That's what got me started with Minecraft animation, and it eventually led me into a lot of the other things I make today.

WHERE IT LED

One project turned into another.

Animation eventually led me toward other kinds of creative work: games, programming, 3D printing, web development, and experiments with hardware. The tools changed, but the basic reason stayed the same — I wanted to make things.
