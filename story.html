WHY I WANTED TO ANIMATE

I wanted to make the stories myself.

Minecraft was the starting point. Animation became the way to turn ideas into something I could actually see.

THE START

Watching wasn't enough.

I got interested in Minecraft animation because I wanted to create my own scenes and stories. Minecraft already gave me a world to build in; animation gave me control over what happened inside it.

That meant learning how characters move, how shots work, how to build a scene, and how to make an idea feel like an actual story.

WHERE IT LED

One project turned into another.

Animation eventually led me toward other kinds of creative work: games, programming, 3D printing, web development, and experiments with hardware. The tools changed, but the basic reason stayed the same — I wanted to make things.
